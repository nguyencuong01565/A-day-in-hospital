🏣 A-day-in-hospital

A Day in Hospital is a dataset about patient satisfaction surveys based on 10 service aspects of healthcare facilities across the United States. The survey evaluates patients’ satisfaction levels during their hospital stay

1.Dataset
- 40,000+ survey responses
- Coverage: 50 U.S. states
- Source data distributed across 7 CSV files

2.Tools
- Excel (EDA, Pivot Tables, Charts)
- Power BI (Data Modeling & Visualization)
- DAX (Calculated Measures)

3.Workflow
- Conducted exploratory data analysis in Excel to understand data structure, distributions, and key metrics.
- Imported and transformed data in Power BI.
- Created DAX measures and KPIs for performance evaluation.
- Developed interactive dashboards for state-level and national-level analysis.
- Generated recommendations based on the lowest-rated service dimensions.

**4.Key Findings**
- Identified the three lowest-rated healthcare service dimensions across the United States, highlighting areas requiring immediate quality improvement initiatives.
- Survey participation declined by approximately 30% over a five-year period, potentially reducing the reliability and representativeness of patient satisfaction measurements.
- All 10 metrics showed a consistent downward trend (minor) during the most recent two years, indicating a gradual deterioration in overall patient experience nationwide.

**5.Business Recommendations**
- Prioritize the bottom 30% of states with the lowest survey response rates and establish a target of at least 500 responses/year per state to improve data reliability.
- Implement nationwide patient-care service improvement and staff training programs if the downward trend across all satisfaction metrics continues over the next 1–2 years.
- Develop targeted action plans for states performing worst in the three lowest-rated service dimensions, focusing resources on the specific areas identified in the analysis (details in report).
